# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Accessible & Helpful:** Our communication is clear, straightforward, and supportive. We aim to demystify complex security and privacy concepts, making them understandable for all stakeholders while maintaining professionalism.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Complexity:** We prioritize intuitive navigation and clear data visualization. The user interface should reduce cognitive load, allowing security teams to focus on identifying and managing risks without being overwhelmed by data density.

## Documentation and Error Handling
- **Educational & Explanatory:** We go beyond reporting facts. Documentation and error messages should explain the why behind PII detection, helping users build a deeper understanding of privacy risks and the reasoning of the AI models.
